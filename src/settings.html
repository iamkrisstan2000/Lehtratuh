App mode

Material

The surface behind the window. Acrylic blurs, Mica tints the wallpaper.

Lite mode is on: this display cannot render a window backdrop, so the app is painting flat to stay fast. Acrylic and Mica come back automatically on a machine with display acceleration.

Accent colour

Applies to buttons, sliders, selection and highlights. System follows your Windows accent and updates when you change it.
